Printable word search generator

Type your own words, pick a size and print. The puzzle and its answer key come out clean on one A4 sheet each — free, no sign-up, and nothing leaves your browser.

One word per line, or separated by commas. Letters and spaces only — hyphens are removed. Up to 30 words.

How to make a printable word search

  1. Type your words. A class spelling list, party guest names, vocabulary from a lesson — anything. One word per line works best.
  2. Choose a grid. Leave it on Auto and the grid grows until every word fits. Pick a fixed size when you need all puzzles in a set to look the same.
  3. Generate, then print. The puzzle and its answer key are laid out for A4 with room for margins, so nothing gets cut off by the printer.

What this generator does differently

Most free generators bury the print button, add a watermark, or ask you to sign up before you can see the puzzle. This one is a single page that runs entirely offline in your browser: your words are never uploaded, there is no account, and the print stylesheet is built for real paper — black letters, generous margins, one puzzle per sheet, and an answer key that shows exactly where each word sits.

Good words to use

  • Short and varied — mix 4-letter and 8-letter words. All 3-letter words makes a puzzle that solves itself.
  • Themed sets — 10 to 15 words on one topic reads better than 30 random ones.
  • Watch repeated letters — words that share letters are harder to find and more fun.

Difficulty, without extra settings

Two toggles cover it: turning off diagonals makes an easy puzzle for young children, and turning on backwards makes an adult puzzle noticeably harder. Leave diagonals on and backwards off for the middle setting that suits most family puzzles.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no limit on how many puzzles you make.

Do you store my words?

No. The page works without a network connection once it has loaded, and the words are only used in your browser's memory.

Can I use the puzzles commercially?

The puzzles you generate with your own words are yours — classrooms, parties, church bulletins, work newsletters. Please don't resell the generator itself.

Why are some of my words missing?

Words that are too long for the grid, or that repeat a word already placed, are listed under the puzzle. Either shorten them or choose a larger grid.
