Free tools

Small tools that do one job well

Najura Apps are free browser tools from the studio behind our puzzle games. No accounts, no uploads, no sign-up walls — everything runs on your own device. The first one is ready: a printable word search generator for classrooms, parties and rainy afternoons.

Printable word search generator
Why these tools exist

Made the same way as our games

We build things we would want to use ourselves: one clear task, a clean interface, and no reason to hand over your data. Every tool on this site works without an account, and most of them keep working with no connection at all. When a tool needs your words or an image, that information stays in your browser — it is never uploaded to a server.

More tools are on the way, including classroom worksheet helpers and printable puzzle sheets that match the games on Najura Games.
